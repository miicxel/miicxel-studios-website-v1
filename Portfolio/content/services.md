## What Short-Form Repurposing Actually Means

Repurposing is not "cut it shorter."
It is finding the 45 seconds inside an hour that matter.
Then rebuilding that moment so it stands alone.

### 1. Sourcing the Raw Material
Podcasts, YouTube videos, webinars, livestreams, talks, coaching calls, interviews.
Raw Zoom footage is fine.
The value is in the words, not the polish.

### 2. Finding the Moments
Not every minute is worth clipping.
We look for one clear point.
We look for a hook in the first seconds.
We look for an arc that finishes cleanly.

### 3. Structuring the Cut
Tighter pacing.
Dead air removed.
A hook in the first 1-3 seconds.
A clear through-line.
An ending that lands or opens a loop.

### 4. Visual Treatment
Vertical reframe.
Captions styled to your brand.
B-roll and graphics that support the point.
Emphasis used on purpose, never as a crutch.

### 5. Sound Design
Clean audio.
Music that fits the moment.
Sound effects used sparingly.

### 6. Platform-Specific Formatting
Reels and TikTok: fast hook, tight pacing.
YouTube Shorts: a different pacing tolerance.
LinkedIn: more restrained, professional tone.

### 7. Output and Delivery
Organized, labeled, ready to publish.
No sorting through an unlabeled folder.
