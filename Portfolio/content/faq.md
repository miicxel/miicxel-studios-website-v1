# FAQ

---

### What content do you need from me to get started?

Any long-form talking content.
Podcasts, YouTube videos, webinars, livestreams, calls with permission, or recorded talks.
Raw Zoom footage is fine.
The value is in what's said, not production polish.

### How does the process work?

You send footage on a regular cadence.
We find the strongest moments, cut, caption, and format them.
Clips come back ready to post.

### What's the turnaround time?

Clips delivered within 3 business days of receiving footage.

### How many revisions are included?

One round included per clip. Additional rounds billed separately.

### Do you handle posting and scheduling?

The base package gives you finished, platform-ready files.
Posting and scheduling is an add-on.

### What if I don't have much long-form content yet?

Even one episode can be enough.
The right moments matter more than volume.

### Is there a minimum commitment?

No.
We work month to month.
No long-term contract.

### How do you handle confidentiality?

Source footage and unreleased material stay confidential.
We never use or share it outside your project.

### What if a clip doesn't perform?

Not every clip will hit.
That is fine.
What matters is the pattern over time.
That is why this works best as an ongoing retainer.

### How do we get started?

Reach out and tell us what you post now.
We'll talk through your first batch before anything is booked.
