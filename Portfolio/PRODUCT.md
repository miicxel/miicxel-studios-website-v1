# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Framer Motion — chosen by the client in the build brief; standard Next.js, static-export friendly. Fonts: Space Grotesk (display/body) + IBM Plex Mono (timecodes, metadata). Videos are 9:16 (1080×1920).

## Users

Coaches, consultants, course creators, and personal brands who already talk for a living (podcasts, calls, webinars, talks) and need that long-form talking turned into a steady stream of short-form clips (Shorts, Reels, TikToks) without adding editing to their own workload. They have no time to edit, post inconsistently, or sit on unused long-form footage.

## Product Purpose

Miicxel Studios turns long-form talking content into a steady stream of high-performing short-form video, ready to post. Success for the client: consistent posting with zero editing effort; success for the site: qualifying leads who book a conversation about their first batch of clips.

## Positioning

A premium, quiet, operational repurposing infrastructure — built around a single dedicated editor (Mickel), not a rotating agency pool; the client stays the face of the brand. Explicitly NOT a social media agency: no ads, no community management, no general marketing. Works only with an existing person/voice at the center of a brand; not for scripted studio-style content.

## Operating Context

Retainer-based, month-to-month, no long-term contract. Source long-form footage flows in on a weekly or biweekly cadence; finished platform-ready clips flow back out on the same rhythm (turnaround: 2-3 business days). Raw Zoom recordings and ring-light setups are acceptable sources. Engagement is direct with the editor — no account manager layer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core: short-form repurposing — sourcing raw material, finding the moments, structuring the cut, visual treatment (vertical 9:16 reframe, captions, b-roll), sound design, platform-specific formatting (Reels/TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn), organized delivery.
- Add-ons: publishing & scheduling; custom caption & brand styling; extended volume/higher cadence; multi-hook variants; thumbnail/cover frame design; monthly performance recap.
- One round of revisions included per clip (entry packages); unlimited for higher tiers.
- Pricing: single clip $79-149; monthly Starter $499/8 clips, Growth $749/15 clips (featured), Pro $999/20 clips; these are starting rates that increase incrementally.
- [ADJUST] markers in content/faq.md for turnaround, revisions, and minimum commitment must be resolved by the client before launch — do not fabricate numbers.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Miicxel Studios. Founder: Mickel.
- Voice: premium, quiet, direct, operational; "invisible infrastructure behind your brand."
- Visual direction is pinned by branding/style.md: dark-only, tinted near-black `#0B0B0E` (never pure black), flat violet accent `#7C3AED` (never gradients, never ambient glows), Space Grotesk + monospace pairing (never Inter), timeline/scrubber vocabulary (scroll-progress playhead scrubber is the signature element, timecode section labels instead of numbers), hairline dividers instead of nested cards, controlled non-overshoot easing, no stock photos, no rounded icon tiles above headings.
- Reference dislikes (references/sites-i-like.md): loud gradients, neon colors, cheesy agency sites, fake growth counters, stock photos.
- Anti-pattern list in branding/style.md is binding.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof — real footage leads; claims are made once and demonstrated.
2. One editor, one consistent eye — the site should read as a single craftsperson's shop, not an agency.
3. System over output — consistent cadence is the product, not individual clips.
4. Quiet luxury — premium restraint; nothing loud, glowing, or decorative.
5. Client is the face — infrastructure is invisible; the site sells the result, not the process spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements; respects prefers-reduced-motion (motion config + media query); semantic HTML throughout; captions/subs visible in video previews where present; contrast per pinned palette (light text on tinted near-black).
